## Profil divisi dan worker

### 101. Outbound Prospecting Intelligence

- **ID:** `outbound_prospecting_intelligence`
- **Misi:** Memprioritaskan akun target untuk outreach berdasarkan kecocokan layanan dan bukti kebutuhan.
- **Tanggung jawab:** Memprioritaskan akun target untuk outreach berdasarkan kecocokan layanan dan bukti kebutuhan.; Score fit, value, urgency, risk, and confidence using approved AIGROWTH criteria; Maintain owner, status, evidence source, SLA, and next action; Log exceptions and escalate blocked or high-risk decisions
- **Input:** CRM and existing prospect/opportunity history; Authorized source evidence for Outbound Prospecting Intelligence; Approved AIGROWTH service catalog, capacity, and commercial guardrails
- **Output final divisi:** Ranked target accounts with fit score, evidence, public contact route, and next action
- **Output tambahan:** Prioritized work queue with owner, status, SLA, and next action; Evidence trail, exceptions, and measurable results for Owner Dashboard
- **KPI:** Qualified targets; contact validity; positive reply rate
- **Automation level:** `assisted`
- **Approval policy:** Use evidence-backed analysis and authorized sources; record provenance, timestamps, confidence, exceptions, and recommended next action. External commitments require Owner approval.

**4 AI worker roles — status planned:**

1. **Research & Intake** — Collect and validate authorized source inputs, preserve provenance, and flag missing or stale fields. *Output:* Validated input bundle with source evidence and missing-data flags.
2. **Analysis & Prioritization** — Analyze inputs against the division mission, scoring criteria, expected value, urgency, and risk. *Output:* Evidence-backed scorecard with priority, confidence, and recommended next action.
3. **Workflow & Handoff** — Prepare the structured workflow task, handoff, or approval request; external actions require approval and a live executor. *Output:* Structured task or handoff with owner, due time, approval requirement, and blocked reason if needed.
4. **Quality & Reporting** — Validate output quality and policy compliance, log exceptions, and report the measurable outcome. *Output:* Quality-checked result, exception log, KPI status, and next-step handoff.; Final handoff summary for the division and Owner Dashboard
